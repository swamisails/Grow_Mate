Run date: 2026-10-30. About 20 tool calls; 14 sources read directly or via search summaries.

## Leads
- **Chronus Vendr median ($11.6k)** conflicts with Chronus's enterprise positioning and with the MentorcliQ median ($29.5k). Check Chronus's own pricing page plus G2/Capterra. Report both figures; don't average.
- **Vendr data freshness:** Degreed, MentorcliQ and Chronus pages carry no date or n. Check whether Vendr's "Ruth" or Price Checks can supply dated figures.
- **PushFar:** the pricing URL is 404 after a domain move to pushfar.app. Find the new pricing page (historically it had published tiers; I haven't verified that this run).
- **Fuel50, Workday (Talent Marketplace / Skills Cloud / Learning) and SAP SuccessFactors (Opportunity Marketplace / Growth Portfolio)** weren't researched. These matter most if the company already runs Workday or SAP: the "adopt an add-on" path may be the cheapest buy option.
- **Together's per-active-participant billing** vs **MentorcliQ's employee bands** vs **360Learning's active-user billing**: model the per-seat growth risk at 500, 2,000 and 10,000 employees.
- **Bersin's May 2026 shift** toward "build agents on vendor platforms" (Eightfold TalentForge, Gloat MCP): a hybrid third option between build and buy that's worth exploring in round 2.
- The vendor claim that "only 3 of 150+ companies successfully built and maintained" an in-house tool (seen in build-vs-buy search results, publisher unclear) is a vendor marketing statistic. Trace the original or discard it.
- Fetch the original **Zylo 2026 SaaS Management Index** and a primary **Vendr SaaS Trends** report for renewal-uplift magnitude (we have frequency, 79%, but magnitude only from blogs).
- Build-side: look for primary benchmarks (Standish CHAOS, Gartner IT key metrics, internal-tool case studies such as a company engineering blog describing a homegrown mentoring or skills tool).

- **Analyst (Gartner, RedThread) primary data** on build-vs-buy outcomes for internal HR or talent tools. Not found this run. Bersin offers opinion, not outcome data.
- **Primary (non-agency) benchmarks** for custom HR/LMS build cost, team size, or MVP timeline. Only agency estimates were found. Team size and time to MVP weren't found in any source this run.
- **Quantified hidden build costs** (security review, WCAG accessibility, SSO/HRIS integration, question-bank authoring, admin FTE). Only the agency "20 to 40% extra" figure was found.
- **Dates on vendor pricing pages and on Vendr medians**: none shown except Credly (Feb 2026).
