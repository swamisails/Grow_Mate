Fit read-out:
- No product read this run demonstrates R3+R4+R5 together: tiered assessments with pass marks that gate mentor status, plus a retake cooldown. The nearest are ServiceNow (verified skill proficiency weights matching [9]) and SkillsTX (assessment-driven matching [20]). Both rank mentors; neither gates them.
- R1/R2/R9 core mentoring flows are partially evidenced across the mentoring vendors. Mentorloop has the best-documented capacity control [19].
- R6 is evidenced natively only by Together (LinkedIn sharing [7]). Credly-grade verified credentials come from an integration (Degreed [15], CredSpark/Skilljar/Workato pattern [21]).
- Buying would most likely mean a combination: a mentoring platform (R1/R2/R7/R9), an assessment layer with tiered pass marks and retake rules (R3/R5), Credly (R6), and custom glue for R4 eligibility gating. That glue is the piece no vendor showed.

## Leads

- **Consolidation is the dominant 2024–2026 trajectory:** LMS/LXP vendors are absorbing mentoring point solutions. Absorb bought Together (2024-12) [5]; Thrive bought Guider (2025-07) [11]; Chronus bought Imperative and MentorStrat (2024) [10]; MentorcliQ bought Diverst (2023) [4]. Round 2 should check whether standalone Together and Guider products are being sunset or kept (Guider is "standalone" per [11]; Together is "embedded within Absorb LMS" per [8]).
- **Absorb domain move** (absorblms.com → absorbai.com, observed 2026-10-30): confirm whether it is a rebrand and what it means for the Together product roadmap.
- **Gloat distress signal** [13]: a 20% layoff, customer non-renewals and an AI-agent pivot. Talent marketplaces may be a weaker category to bet on; check Workday, SAP Opportunity Marketplace and Fuel50 for similar signals.
- **ServiceNow Mentoring + Skills Foundation** [9] is the most "skills-verification-aware" mentoring product found. Read its "Configuring Mentoring" docs (capacity, accept/decline, notifications, admin assignment) and check whether Skills Foundation supports assessments with levels. This is relevant if the company already runs ServiceNow.
- **Workday Talent Marketplace / Skills Cloud and SAP SuccessFactors Opportunity Marketplace (mentoring module):** not read this run. SAP's Mentoring may have mentor capacity and approval flows. Check SAP help portal and Workday docs.
- **Chronus features:** no product or help pages read. Chronus is a major enterprise player with a $78M PE investment [10] and is a priority for round 2. Verify the Rumi/$78M claims against chronus.com press releases.
- **Assessment layer:** check iMocha, TestGorilla or CredSpark for configurable pass marks, multiple levels and retake cooldowns (R3/R5). Look for an LMS quiz engine (Absorb, Cornerstone, Docebo) with "retake delay" settings. LMS quiz engines commonly have attempt limits; verify.
- **Credly ownership/trajectory** (Pearson) and Open Badges 3.0 support were not checked.
- **Contradiction:** Gloat's March 2026 layoff is 20% (Globes) vs "35 people / 35%" (layoffhedge tracker). Globes is preferred.
- **Contradiction:** Together's acquisition date is 2024-12-10 (MergerLinks/CB Insights) vs 2024-12-19 (Fasken). A WCAS release is shown dated 2024-01-06, likely a mis-dated listing.
- **New candidate entities:** ServiceNow Mentoring, Thrive (+Guider), Absorb (+Together), SkillsTX, CredSpark, Mentor Collective (seen in comparisons; not checked).
