## Leads

- **Mentor gating vs. supply (critical for this design).** All supply evidence (claims 13–15, 12) shows mentors are already scarce. A three-level test with Gold required to mentor, plus a one-month retake wait, would further restrict supply. No source tested this. Round 2 should look for evidence on *mentor pool size ratios* (mentor:mentee), and on MentorcliQ's or Together's own reports of how many applicants become mentors.
- **Matching input contradiction.** The planned admin-assigned group mentoring conflicts with Allen, Eby & Lentz (2006), where participant input predicted outcomes. Fetch the original paper and Eby 2008 to confirm effect sizes. Also check the claim that "informal > formal" mentoring.
- **Clutterbuck 1-in-3 vs 2-in-3 training statistic.** Widely recycled. Find the primary source. If it holds, it suggests mentor *training* rather than mentor *testing* is the evidence-backed quality lever.
- **Future Skills Centre 2025 PDF.** It couldn't be parsed. Re-fetch for participation rates and the reasons people don't participate.
- **MentorcliQ "Mentoring Impact Report" / ATD mentoring research.** These are the upstream sources for the 98% figure and retention claims. Read directly and label as vendor data.
- **Internal (not hiring) value of badges.** All badge evidence found is about *external hiring*. Round 2: does putting internal verified skills on resumes affect internal mobility? See Degreed/Workday skills-cloud case studies and LinkedIn Workplace Learning Report 2025.
- **Stale skill profiles.** No quantitative source was found on how fast self-reported skill profiles go stale. Try the Gartner/Josh Bersin "skills tech" research and the Deloitte skills-based organisation survey.
- **Direct review reading.** G2/Capterra blocked fetch. Try TrustRadius, Gartner Peer Insights (gartner.com/reviews/market/mentoring-software) or Software Advice for fuller, dated 1–3★ text.

## Looked for, not found

- **Evidence that any organisation qualifies mentors through a multi-level *skill assessment* or test.** None found. Practice is self-assessment questionnaires (Chronus), experience plus professional certification (education and professional bodies), or characteristics-based selection (Deng & Turner 2024). This absence is a finding: the planned Gold-badge gating appears unprecedented, and its effect on supply is unstudied.
- **Evidence on retake waiting periods for internal skill assessments,** or their effect on engagement. Not searched in depth; nothing surfaced.
- **Low-star (1–3★) review text for Mentorloop and PushFar.** None surfaced, and review volume is low. Mentoring-software reviews are overwhelmingly 4–5★.
- **Reddit practitioner threads complaining about corporate mentoring software.** The query returned only Reddit's own internal programme posts. No usable complaint threads.
- **Gartner Peer Insights and TrustRadius critical reviews.** Not reached within budget.
- **Recent (≤2 years) peer-reviewed data on mentoring's effect on retention and promotion.** Only older meta-analyses (2006–2008) and vendor-cited figures were found.
- **Quantified data on skills-taxonomy maintenance cost or skill-profile staleness rates.** Only qualitative vendor and consultancy statements were found.
- **Employer or employee valuation of *internally* issued skill badges** (as opposed to external hiring credentials). Not found.
