# GrowMate

Course Completed is part of the idea. The mentor judges this person ready for the assessment. It does not mean the sessions are over. The learner can wait for that. The click only opens the assessment.

A live match ends only when someone quits or the mentor clicks Course Completed. The product does not cancel it.

A person becomes a mentor for a course in one of three ways:

- Pass gold. No Admin approval. Bronze and silver do not.
- At registration, name more than one course. Admin decides, course by course. Approved courses are requestable immediately. Refused courses are not. A refused course returns only if they later choose to pass gold. Registration is not rerun.
- Admin assigns anyone to a group on a course. They become a mentor for that course only when they Accept. If they never Accept, they are not a mentor for it. After Accept, each person in the group is their own match.

Rejected: gold as the only door. Mentorship granted at registration without Admin. One course only. One stamp for the whole registration list. Assignment limited to people who are already mentors. Assignment applying only to the group. Course mentorship starting when Admin selects them. A timer that ends a live match.
