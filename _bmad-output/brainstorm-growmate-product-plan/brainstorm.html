GrowMate · 24 October 2026 · partner session, kept loose

The course outlives the mentor.

A person can step off. The course stays on the path, and someone else can be accepted onto it.

Brought into the room

A product plan so employees can learn skills, find mentors, pass assessments, earn badges, and put verified skills and certificates on resumes.

From the readme they started with: Employee and Admin; learn, mentor, a three-level assessment, a Gold badge, a one-month reattempt wait, certificates on resumes, notifications.

Still standing at the close

GrowMate is a match-and-proof loop. Courses are published. A mentor is accepted. Talks stay outside the app. Course Completed opens bronze, then silver, then gold. Gold is only the right to mentor that course.

One Accept, then many matches

At the table

Coach

A group assignment and a one-to-one request are the same door. After Accept, the match forgets how it started.

The picture they said was fine

A match-and-proof loop.

The coach had offered one loop as the thing to prove. What was confirmed is this, without a score and without a prize.

  1. Courses are published.
  2. A mentor is accepted.
  3. Talks stay outside the app.
  4. Course Completed opens bronze, then silver, then gold.
  5. Gold is only the right to mentor that course.
  6. Admin group assignment only creates many one-to-one matches at once.

Quit is normal, and the course outlives any one mentor.

Quit is the only place a reason is written. After Accept, a match forgets whether it began as one request or as one of a group. The person who left can ask again. The course is still published.
