topic: Learners get a Mentor and are judged ready
updated: 2026-11-10T16:42

- (requirement) R1 (parent R7): A Learner sends a Request, with no reason, to one Mentor listed on a published Course. It is refused, with a specific reason code, when the Learner already has a pending Request or live Match on that Course, Course Completed has been clicked for them there, or they are a Mentor for it. One shared eligibility check serves Requests and Group assignment.
- (requirement) R2 (parent R8): Every Employee has a Requests screen listing the pending Requests sent to them, each as "[Learner] requested you to guide them with [Course]" with Accept as the only response. Accept creates a Match and sets the Learner's status to In progress. A Mentor's live Matches are unlimited.
- (requirement) R3 (parent R9): An un-Accepted Request is reminded to both parties exactly 72 hours after it was sent and Cancelled exactly 144 hours after, and Accept is refused from then on. After a Cancel the Learner has no Progress status on that Course unless they have Stage progress there. Nothing else happens on any other day, and Admin receives nothing.
- (requirement) R4 (parent R9; architecture AD-5, AD-6): The time rules are worked out from stored timestamps against one clock, with no scheduled jobs. In the `e2e` environment only, a test clock sets the current time, and the Playwright harness has a helper to set it; `local` and `production` have no test clock.
- (requirement) R5 (parent R12): Both parties of a live Match can open its Match screen: the other person's name, the Course and the bar. The Learner can only Quit Course; the Mentor can click Course Completed or Quit Course. There is no chat, call, file sharing, helper text, activity indicator, Mentor route or group origin. An ended Match has no screen and disappears from Home.
- (requirement) R6 (parent R13): Either party can Quit a live Match at once with a required reason of 1 to 500 characters and no approval. The other party receives the reason as an in-app note. The Learner's status becomes Quit, whoever quit, and the Learner can request the Course again.
- (requirement) R7 (parent R14): Only the Mentor can click Course Completed, after a confirmation. It ends the Match, makes Bronze available, and notifies the Learner. The Learner stays In progress, and nothing else is granted.
- (requirement) R8 (parent R15): A live Match never ends by timer or inactivity, only by Quit or Course Completed.
- (requirement) R9 (parent R20): The bar shows Not yet started (grey) for a pending Request, In progress (half blue) from Accept, and Quit (red) until a new Request is sent, with its state word and nothing else. It appears on Home's Learning card, the Course page, the Match screen and the Upskilling list. A Learner with none of these states has no bar.
- (requirement) R10 (parent R21): Any signed-in user, Admin included, can view one Upskilling list of every Employee In progress on some Course, with name, Course and status, sorted by name. It has no scores, ranking or actions.
- (requirement) R11 (parent R22): The product sends these notices, never to Admin and never revealing a Mentor route or group origin: new Request (to the Mentor), Accept (to the Learner), the day-3 reminder and day-6 Cancel (to both, worked out from the Request and never stored), the Quit note (to the other party) and Bronze available (to the Learner).
- (requirement) R12 (EXPERIENCE.md, Information Architecture): Home shows the Learning card (each Course where the Employee has a status, with its bar and a link to the Match or Course) and the Mentoring card (their live Matches as Mentor, hidden when there are none).
