### Architecture delta

The API's import graph has only the allowed edges (Courses→Accounts, Mentors→Accounts/Courses/Notifications, Notifications→Accounts). No module queries or joins another module's tables, no controller checks `is_admin` inline, no Resource can emit a Mentor `route`, and `GrantMentor` is the only writer of `mentor_routes`. Checked and clean on those points.
