# Design Thinking Session: GROW_MATE

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Facilitator:** Sitadevi.padala
**Design Challenge:** The whole GrowMate loop for learners, mentors, and Admin

---

## 🎯 Design Challenge

Design the GrowMate loop so a learner can get a mentor, finish a course, and earn the right to mentor that course by passing gold, while Admin can approve named courses at registration and assign a group to anyone. Talks stay outside the app. Admin does not gain features beyond the ones already decided. The session uses both the forged idea and the earlier brainstorm.

**Users:** learners, mentors, and Admin.

**Constraints:** no in-app chat; Admin limited to publishing courses, uploading bronze/silver/gold assessments and pass marks, the shared upskilling list, approving or refusing named registration courses, and assigning a group.

**Success:** a learner is matched, the mentor judges them ready, gold makes them a mentor for that course, and Admin can staff mentors through registration approval and group assignment.

## 👥 EMPATHIZE: Understanding Users

### User Insights

Journey map, marked by Sitadevi.padala. These are not quotes from employees in the room.

The learner struggles most while talking outside the app, because nothing from those talks is recorded here. The mentor struggles most when judging "ready," because those same talks are not in the app. Admin struggles most when approving some named courses and refusing others.

The learner and the mentor share one pain: the work that matters happens somewhere else. Admin's pain is earlier, at registration, and it is a decision with no score.

### Key Observations

- The match looks empty during the only part where learning actually happens.
- Course Completed asks the mentor to judge readiness from memory of talks the product never saw.
- A registration refusal is personal and uneven: some of a person's courses proceed, others do not, and gold is the only way back.
- Admin is not struggling with the request flow. They were kept out of it on purpose.

### Empathy Map Summary

**Learner.** Says the talks happened elsewhere. Thinks the app does not know the work. Keeps talking outside, then returns to a match that still looks unfinished. Feels unsure the effort counts.

**Mentor.** Says they must judge readiness. Thinks the click has no record behind it. Clicks Course Completed from the outside conversations. Feels the weight of a judgment the app cannot show.

**Admin.** Says yes to some named courses and no to others. Thinks there is no score to lean on. Splits the list. Feels the decision is theirs alone.

## 🎨 DEFINE: Frame the Problem

### Point of View Statement

**Learner.** A learner needs the live match to stay half blue, with no numbers, until the mentor clicks, because the talks happen outside GrowMate and that unchanged wait is normal.

**Mentor.** A mentor needs Course Completed to mean "I judge this person ready for the assessment," because that click is the record and it opens the assessment.

**Admin.** Admin needs to approve some named registration courses and refuse others, with no score beside the decision, because the choice is theirs alone. A refused course returns only if that person later chooses to pass gold.

### How Might We Questions

- How might we hold a live match on the half-blue in-progress bar until the mentor clicks Course Completed or someone quits?
- How might we open bronze from that click, and keep silver and gold hidden until the previous stage is cleared?
- How might we give every mentor the same bar, including a person who just passed gold?
- How might we tell the learner only who accepted, and for which course?
- How might we let Admin decide the registration list course by course, so approved courses can be requested immediately?
- How might we keep the shared upskilling list as a list of people who are upskilling?

### Key Insights

- The journey-map pains are the product working as decided. The empty middle, the click with no transcript, and Admin's bare yes or no are normal.
- Half blue means in progress. It does not mean the course content is halfway done. The bar has no numbers: empty grey, half blue, full green, quit red.
- A live match ends only when someone quits or the mentor clicks Course Completed. The product does not cancel it.
- On accept the learner sees one line: the mentor's name and the course. The door that made the mentor (gold, registration approval, or Admin assignment) stays off that screen. After Accept, the match forgets whether it began as one request or as one of a group.
- Quit is the only place a reason is written. The request itself has no reason. Bronze and silver give nothing of their own. Gold is the right to mentor that course.
