Create a complete product plan for GrowMate, an Employee Skill Development and Mentorship Platform.
The platform helps employees learn new skills, connect with experienced mentors, complete assessments, earn badges, and add verified skills and certificates to their resumes.
1. User Roles
There are two roles: Employee and Admin. Employees can act as learners or mentors based on their skills and eligibility.
2. Employee Features
Signup: Name, email, primary skills, and years of experience.
Profile: Manage primary skills, learning interests, badges, certificates, and mentorship availability.
Learn New Skills: Browse skills, select a skill, find available mentors, and send mentorship requests.
Mentorship: Experienced employees can express interest in becoming mentors, specify their availability (months and hours per day), receive requests, and accept or reject them.
Mentor Eligibility: Employees must pass a three-level assessment to earn a Gold badge and qualify as mentors.
Restriction: Prevent mentor-to-mentor requests for the same skill.
Learning Progress: Track courses, mentorship sessions, assessments, and completed skills.
Assessments: Three levels of tests, with a Gold badge awarded after successful completion.
Reattempts: Define assessment retry rules with a one-month waiting period.
Certificates: Generate certificates and allow employees to add verified skills and certificates to their resumes.
Notifications: Notify employees about mentorship requests, approvals, rejections, assessments, and achievements.
3. Admin Features
Admin Signup: Name and email.
Dashboard: Overview of employees, mentors, learners, pending requests, and assessments.
Employee Management: View employees, primary skills, upskilling interests, badges, and certificates. Provide filters.
Mentorship Approvals: Review and approve pending mentor applications.
Mentor Management: Add mentors, assign employees, and manage mentor availability.
Assessment Management: Create and manage skills, tests, questions, levels, passing criteria, and reattempt rules.
Progress Tracking: Monitor employee learning progress, mentorship activity, and achievements.
